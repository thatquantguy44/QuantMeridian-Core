## What each release includes

R1 is useful portfolio research before HMMs or LLMs. It includes source and
knowledge-basis labels, a genuine service-down/unconfigured state and verifiable
numerics. It remains a local session preview; exported files do not provide
durable central history. R2 completes the requested notebook-inspired breadth,
including a first long/flat unlevered variant of the signal strategies. Original
short and 2× leveraged variants remain outside that release until financed/borrowed
cash and permission contracts exist.

R2 first supports small bounded interactive experiments; full grid/long history
uses the supervised offline model CLI. If the owner requires seamless long jobs
in the GUI, move the approved durable-job subset of R4 ahead of that experience.
Do not stretch synchronous deadlines or claim transient jobs survive restart.

R3 can be cut without removing any numerical model or export. Domain instructions
already shape design, but runtime assistance has separate trust, dispatch and
provider readiness. R4 may proceed independently from R3; a good LLM narrative is
neither a source-admission prerequisite nor operational evidence.

No broker connection, automatic trade, investment recommendation approval,

part of the initial roadmap commitment.

## Release decisions and measures

Measure usable coverage, failed-fit/invalid-input rate, numerical residuals,
replay success, runtime/RSS, paired net-return/risk/cost results and user ability to
trace a claim to its source. Model count, attractive notebook charts and LLM prose
are not release metrics. Keep economic promotion thresholds prespecified and
distinct from engineering acceptance; a model that loses to a baseline can still
be valid exploratory research.

Estimate dates and cost only after model ownership, data availability and target
host are accepted and the R1 benchmark establishes actual work size. Pause a
dependent capability when its decision/source gate is unresolved while advancing
independent approved tasks. Model rollback restores an exact prior binding;
source failures remain visible, never a trigger for automatic synthetic fallback.
