# Relationship Intelligence Graph roadmap

## R0 — synthetic relationship explorer

Bounded fictional entities, two synthetic books, collateral schedules, graph
edges, coupling matrix, scenario delta review, evidence ledger,
inventory-to-obligation explorer, impact-path drillthrough and bounded temporal
graph replay. Exit: KGR-T01 through KGR-T10 with
deterministic fixture tests and browser verification.

## R1 — source-backed schedule research proposal

Admit point-in-time collateral eligibility, haircut, margin and substitution
schedules only after source rights, document identity, effective-date semantics,
versioning, freshness and unit normalization are approved. Preserve the original
document reference and normalized clause rather than replacing one with the other.

The [six-provider research package](SOURCE_ADMISSION.md) prepares CME, LCH Ltd,
ICE Clear Europe, Eurex, Bank of England and Federal Reserve candidates. Research
coverage, incomplete CME original capture and remaining admission gates are
explicit; no live adapter or portable service is activated.

## R2 — graph analytics and ML proposal

Evaluate centrality, community detection, link-change alerts or anomaly models
only after a historical relationship ledger exists. Any model output remains an
estimate with features, training window, held-out evaluation and confidence;
missing edges cannot become negative evidence silently.

## R3 — protected deployment proposal

Measure graph size, query latency, memory, retention, access control and audit
requirements before choosing a graph database or service deployment. No order
routing or automatic collateral movement is in scope.
